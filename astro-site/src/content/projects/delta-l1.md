## Challenge

Integrate onboard sensing and wireless telemetry into a compact, reusable avionics system for a high-power rocket.

## Design

The project combines an ESP32-based telemetry system, GPS and inertial sensing, barometric altitude measurement, and ground-station communications within a compact avionics bay.

## Implementation

- ESP32-based wireless telemetry
- GPS and inertial sensing
- Barometric altitude measurement
- Compact avionics bay packaging
- Ground-station communications

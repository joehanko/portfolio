## Challenge

Build a compact desktop tensile testing machine that brings mechanical design, custom electronics, and embedded control into one open-source project.

## Design

The MACK-10 is designed around controlled linear motion and load-cell force measurement, supported by a custom controller PCB.

## Implementation

- Stepper-driven motion control
- Load-cell force measurement
- Custom controller PCB
- Embedded control and data acquisition

## Controller revision

The controller PCB footprint was reduced by 50% between revisions.

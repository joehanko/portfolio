## Project Overview

The MACK-10 is an open-source desktop tensile testing machine combining mechanical design, custom electronics, and embedded control.

## Engineering Highlights

- Stepper-driven motion control
- Load-cell force measurement
- Custom controller PCB
- 50% reduction in controller PCB footprint between revisions
- Embedded control and data acquisition
